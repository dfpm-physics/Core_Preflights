## What tutorials are

A tutorial is a short guide drawn over the page you are on. It highlights the control to use and a small card beside it says what to do. There are two kinds.

- **Show me around** is a tour of a page. The first time you sign in, and the first time you open a main page, a tour may start by itself.
- **Do it with me** walks you through a real job, such as adding an assignment. You do the job yourself with the page's own buttons, and at the end the real thing exists. These never start by themselves.

## How to start one

Open **Help** and look at **Tutorials** at the top, or choose **Tutorials** from the menu under your name. Press **Start** on the one you want. If it needs a different page, you are taken there and the guide carries on. You can start any tutorial at any time.

## How to leave one

- On a tour, press **End tour**.
- On a walk-through, press **I've got it from here**. The guide closes and the page stays exactly as you have it: nothing you typed is cleared and nothing is undone. You finish the job on your own.

A guide never clicks for you. Where it says to click, you click.

## If a step says it cannot find something

The page may still be loading, or the control may not be there for your role. Press **Try again**, or leave the guide and carry on by yourself.

## Turning automatic tutorials off

Press **Turn off automatic tutorials** on any tour, or open **Account**, **Preferences**, **Automatic tutorials** and choose **Off**. This is saved to your account, so it follows you to any device. Tours stop starting by themselves, and every tutorial stays in Help.

When a new feature arrives with its own tutorial, that one tour shows once, even with automatic tutorials off, and marks itself **New feature**. You can turn them off again from the same card. **Show tours again** on the Account page brings back the tours you have already seen.

## Who sees which

Everyone sees the tours and walk-throughs for their own role. Staff also see the ones for the work below their role. A walk-through that only a site administrator can finish, such as making a new course, tells everyone else who does it and shows them the checklist for their own course.
