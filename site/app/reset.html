Pre-lesson Readiness Engagement Platform

Forgot your password?

PREP cannot email you a reset link — it has no mail server. Password resets happen in person instead.

Cadets: ask any instructor who teaches your section. They can put your password back to the default — the last 6 digits of your cadet ID — and you will choose a new one the moment you sign in. They cannot look up your current password or pick a new one for you.
Instructors: ask a system admin to reset your account.

Already know your password and just want to change it? Sign in first, then open Account from the menu.
